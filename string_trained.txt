Hulas Infra produces durable Solar Top Hat Mounting Structures for large-scale and commercial solar installations. Made from high-quality structural steel with thicknesses of 0.8–2.0 mm and depths of 60–120 mm, the pre-galvanized roll-formed sections are lightweight yet strong, providing efficient load distribution and easy installation. Zinc coatings of 120–275 GSM ensure corrosion, UV, and humidity resistance for long-term performance in diverse climates. Engineered to withstand wind speeds up to 150 km/h and loads up to 1500 N/m², these structures are tested for stability and compatibility with various panel types. Modular and ISO-aligned in manufacturing, they are ideal for utility-scale solar farms, commercial rooftops, and hybrid renewable energy projects, offering quick assembly, alignment accuracy, and minimal maintenance.
Hulas Infra manufactures W-Beam and Thrie-Beam Crash Barriers for road safety across highways, bridges, and mountainous terrains in Nepal. Made from high-strength hot-rolled steel (3.00 mm and 3.43 mm thick) and hot-dip galvanized with up to 550 GSM zinc coating, the barriers resist corrosion, moisture, and harsh weather. Standard beams are 4320 mm long, and fish-tail end sections absorb impact energy to redirect vehicles safely. Galvanized posts, spacer blocks, and high-tensile fasteners complete the system, available in single- and double-sided configurations, conforming to IS 2062 and IRC:119-2015 standards. Designed for durability and low maintenance, these modular barriers can last 25–30 years, providing effective kinetic energy absorption, protecting passengers, vehicles, and infrastructure, with nationwide supply and installation support.
Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) by Hulas Infra are factory-fabricated steel structures that are transported and assembled on-site, offering accuracy, faster timelines, and cost efficiency compared to conventional RCC or steel construction. PEBs are designed for Nepal’s diverse topography and climate, withstanding seismic activity, heavy monsoon rainfall, and high winds. They are permanent, durable solutions suitable for industrial sheds, warehouses, factories, workshops, showrooms, logistics hubs, and institutional projects.
